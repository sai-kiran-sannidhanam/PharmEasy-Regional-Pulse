# Operational Recommendation Memo: Guntur Regional Demand Surge

## Title
Sustaining Post-Spike Fulfillment Capacity and Margin Retention in Guntur [LOW]

## Context
PharmEasy's Telugu-speaking operations desk oversees secondary pharmaceutical distribution across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, with Guntur operating as a key Tier-2 distribution cluster [LOW]. During the April 2026 baseline operating cycle, Guntur recorded 38 orders yielding a total gross revenue of INR 39,268.04 and an operating profit of INR 5,798.24 [HIGH]. Operations were structured around steady-state local courier hand-offs with standard 24–48 hour delivery commitments [LOW].

## Key Insight
Between April and May 2026, Guntur experienced an unprecedented revenue increase of +122.19%, with gross sales surging from INR 39,268.04 to INR 87,249.49 [HIGH]. This commercial acceleration was accompanied by an order volume increase to 75 orders in May 2026 (+97.37% MoM) and a subsequent continuation in June 2026 with 69 orders generating INR 72,130.64 [HIGH].

## Evidence
- **Revenue Trajectory:** April INR 39,268.04 → May INR 87,249.49 (+122.19%) → June INR 72,130.64 (-17.33% stabilization) [HIGH].
- **Order Count Progression:** 38 orders (April) → 75 orders (May) → 69 orders (June), establishing a new operating run-rate ~85% above Q1 baseline [HIGH].
- **Profit Contribution:** Operating profit expanded from INR 5,798.24 in April to INR 13,018.66 in May (+124.53%), confirming margin stability without discounting degradation [HIGH].
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Shifted moderately from INR 1,033.37 in April to INR 1,163.33 in May (+12.58%), indicating that volume growth (order frequency) was the primary expansion driver rather than single-ticket price anomalies [MEDIUM].
- **Desk Comparison:** Over the identical April→May cycle, stable markets like Nellore fluctuated by just -2.18%, proving Guntur's swing is idiosyncratic and not a macro-reporting artifact [HIGH].

## Recommendation
1. **Fulfillment Re-alignment:** Immediately re-allocate secondary distribution replenishment from the Vijayawada regional node to establish a dedicated 7-day safety-stock reserve in Guntur for top-velocity SKU categories (Prescription Medicines and OTC lines) [MEDIUM].
2. **Logistics SLA Protection:** Contract 2 temporary dedicated delivery partners in Guntur to maintain sub-24-hour turnaround times for high-density pincodes during the ongoing higher baseline [LOW].
3. **Margin-Preserving Replenishment:** Restrict fulfillment prioritization to direct-to-pharmacy fast-moving lines to protect the current 14.92% realized net operating margin [MEDIUM].

## Next Check
Conduct a mid-cycle operational review on July 10, 2026, to evaluate June-to-July order velocity, tracking whether order cancellation rates remain below 1.5% and AOV holds above INR 1,100.00 [LOW].
